Opening Trial Terms

  1. The offer is available to first-time visitors to MEG FITNESS Taizhou Road only.
  2. Each person may choose only one of the following: a 7-Day Open Gym Trial, a 50-Minute One-on-One Personal Training Trial, or a 50-Minute One-on-One Reformer Pilates Trial.
  3. Trials cannot be combined, transferred or exchanged for cash.
  4. The claim deadline is September 30, 2026 at 11:00 PM.
  5. A trial is considered successfully claimed only after the user adds the gym manager on WeChat, sends the relevant message and receives confirmation.
  6. The first visit or activation must be completed within 14 calendar days after confirmation.
  7. The Open Gym trial is valid for 7 consecutive calendar days from activation.
  8. Unlimited entries are allowed during the valid period, during opening hours from 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM.
  9. First-time Open Gym activation requires advance booking and staff assistance.
  10. Personal Training and Reformer Pilates trials are 50-minute one-on-one sessions.
  11. Reformer Pilates users may choose a 1-on-2 session if attending with a friend and both agree.
  12. Availability: Open Gym 50 spots; Personal Training 20 spots; Reformer Pilates 20 spots.
  13. Spots are allocated according to the order of WeChat confirmation.
  14. The offer is valid only at MEG FITNESS Taizhou Road.
  15. Booking times may be adjusted depending on staff schedules, equipment maintenance and on-site safety.
