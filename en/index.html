MORE WAYS TO TRAIN

Explore More Training Options

Choose a specialty based on how you move today and what you want to improve next.

Posture Training

Start with an Assessment, Not a Generic Routine

We assess mobility, joint function, movement patterns and side-to-side differences before selecting training that addresses the main limits on everyday movement and exercise.

Good for: stiffness from desk work, posture concerns, restricted movement, noticeable side-to-side differences or exercises that remain difficult despite regular training.

Format: One-on-one Personal Training · Advance booking required

Physical Reconditioning

Rebuild Capacity and Return to Training

Functional assessment, foundational strength, movement control and progressive loading help people gradually regain the capacity for everyday activity and regular training after an injury or long-term exercise-related discomfort.

Good for: returning after an injury, reduced strength, low confidence in movement or a careful return to training load.

Format: One-on-one Personal Training · Advance booking required

Olympic Weightlifting

Snatch, Clean & Jerk and Explosive Strength

Sessions progress from movement foundations and technique practice to strength and power development according to your experience.

Good for: learning Olympic weightlifting technique, developing strength and power or adding a new skill to your training.

Format: One-on-one Personal Training · Led by Coach Xu · Advance booking required

Functional Training

Build Strength That Carries into Movement

Training uses pushing, pulling, squatting, lunging, rotating, carrying and locomotion to improve control, all-round strength and physical ability.

Format: One-on-one Personal Training · Advance booking required

Mobility & Recovery

Restore Usable Range of Motion

Mobility, breathing, active control and recovery work are selected for your current condition—useful after long hours of sitting, hard training or restricted movement.

Format: One-on-one Personal Training · Advance booking required

Sports Performance

Strength, Speed, Power and Sport-Specific Ability

Training may cover strength, speed, change of direction, jumping, throwing and sport-specific movement according to your goals.

Format: One-on-one Personal Training · Advance booking required

Youth Fitness

Build Movement Foundations and Physical Confidence

For children aged 5–12. Age-appropriate sessions develop coordination, balance, speed, agility, foundational strength and movement skills.

Format: One-on-one Personal Training · Advance booking required

Boxing Training

Technique, Fitness and Coordination

Foundational punches, footwork, reaction, coordination and conditioning help develop body control and all-round physical ability.

Format: One-on-one Personal Training · Advance booking required

Group Classes

Structured Training in a Group Setting

Led by the Taizhou Road Gym Manager, with themes such as functional training, strength, body shaping and mobility according to the latest gym schedule.

Format: One-to-many Group Class

Opening Trial Terms

  1. The offer is available to first-time visitors to MEG FITNESS Taizhou Road only.
  2. Each person may choose only one of the following: a 7-Day Open Gym Trial, a 50-Minute One-on-One Personal Training Trial, or a 50-Minute One-on-One Reformer Pilates Trial.
  3. Trials cannot be combined, transferred or exchanged for cash.
  4. The claim deadline is September 30, 2026 at 11:00 PM.
  5. A trial is considered successfully claimed only after the user adds the gym manager on WeChat, sends the relevant message and receives confirmation.
  6. The first visit or activation must be completed within 14 calendar days after confirmation.
  7. The Open Gym trial is valid for 7 consecutive calendar days from activation.
  8. Unlimited entries are allowed during the valid period, during opening hours from 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM.
  9. First-time Open Gym activation requires advance booking and staff assistance.
  10. Personal Training and Reformer Pilates trials are 50-minute one-on-one sessions.
  11. Reformer Pilates users may choose a 1-on-2 session if attending with a friend and both agree.
  12. Availability: Open Gym 50 spots; Personal Training 20 spots; Reformer Pilates 20 spots.
  13. Spots are allocated according to the order of WeChat confirmation.
  14. The offer is valid only at MEG FITNESS Taizhou Road.
  15. Booking times may be adjusted depending on staff schedules, equipment maintenance and on-site safety.
